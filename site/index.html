Product summary

SecureFlow combines deterministic rules, anomaly scoring, and a governed AI reasoning layer to explain findings, rank priorities, and recommend safe actions — always auditable and permissioned.

How it works

  1. Connect systems with secure connectors or CSV uploads
  2. Normalize and analyze events across systems
  3. Detect bottlenecks and anomalous spend
  4. Review explainable recommendations and automate safely

Features

  • Workflow intelligence & bottleneck detection
  • Cross-system event timeline
  • Financial anomaly detection with explainability
  • AI recommendations with confidence scores
  • Human approval gates and audit logs
  • Policy-based safe automation

Security & trust

  • Tenant isolation and role-based access control
  • Auditability and immutable logs
  • Masking/redaction before AI use
  • SCIM/SSO-ready identity provisioning
